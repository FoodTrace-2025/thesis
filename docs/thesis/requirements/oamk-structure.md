### Per-Chapter Word Count Targets

**Use these targets to prevent thesis bloat:**

| Chapter | Target Words | Max Words | Notes |
|---------|--------------|-----------|-------|
| **Ch 1 - Introduction** | 1,000-1,200 | 1,500 | Background + objectives + scope |
| **Ch 2 - Literature Review** | 2,200-2,700 | 3,000 | Synthesize, don't summarize everything |
| **Ch 3 - Methodology** | 1,400-1,700 | 2,000 | Process + justification, not tutorial |
| **Ch 4 - Implementation** | 2,700-3,300 | 4,000 | What you built, key code only |
| **Ch 5 - Testing & Results** | 1,900-2,300 | 2,500 | Results + analysis, detailed tables to appendix |
| **Ch 6 - Discussion** | 1,400-1,700 | 2,000 | Interpret + implications + limitations |
| **Ch 7 - Conclusion** | 1,100-1,300 | 1,500 | Answers + contributions + future work |
| **Abstract + Front Matter** | 500-600 | 800 | Abstract 250-300 words |
| **TOTAL** | **~17,000** | **~19,000** | **DO NOT EXCEED 19,000 WORDS** |

### Length Management Rules

**⚠️ WARNING:** Exceeding word limits demonstrates poor synthesis skills and will impact your grade.

**Best Practices:**
- ✅ **Synthesize, don't summarize** - Compare and contrast sources, don't list them
- ✅ **Move details to appendices** - Full code, detailed tables, raw data
- ✅ **Cut redundancy ruthlessly** - If you've said it once, don't repeat it
- ✅ **One figure = 100 words** - Use visuals to replace text where possible
- ✅ **Focus on "why" not "what"** - Explain decisions, not just describe actions

**Appendix Strategy (Unlimited Length):**
- Appendix A: Complete source code (smart contracts, key components)
- Appendix B: Detailed test results tables
- Appendix C: Additional diagrams and wireframes
- Appendix D: User testing questionnaires and raw data
- Appendix E: Risk assessment matrices

**Remember:** OAMK reviewers prefer **concise and focused** over **comprehensive and bloated**. A 65-page thesis that demonstrates critical thinking will score higher than a 150-page literature dump.
