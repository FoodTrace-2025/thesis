# Story 4.3: Public Landing Page

## Status

**DONE** - Implemented 2025-12-14

## Story

**As a** visitor to FoodTrace,
**I want** to see a landing page that explains the system and provides clear entry points,
**so that** I understand what FoodTrace does and can navigate to the appropriate section (consumer trace or business login).

## Acceptance Criteria

### Hero Section
1. Page accessible at `/` route (public, no authentication required)
2. Full-page design with minimal header (FoodTrace logo + "Login" link only)
3. Headline: "Know Where Your Food Comes From" (or approved alternative)
4. Subheadline: "Blockchain-verified traceability from producer to your table"
5. Two CTA buttons side by side:
   - Primary: "Track a Product" → navigates to `/trace`
   - Secondary: "Business Login" → navigates to `/login`
6. Buttons have minimum 44px touch targets (mobile accessibility)

### How It Works Section
7. Section visible below hero (peek to encourage scrolling)
8. 3-step visual flow showing supply chain:
   - Step 1: Producer (icon: plant/seedling) - "Products registered on blockchain"
   - Step 2: Supply Chain (icon: truck) - "Tracked through distribution"
   - Step 3: Consumer (icon: user/scan) - "Verify with a simple scan"
9. Simple icons from react-icons (no custom images required)
10. Responsive: horizontal on desktop (≥768px), vertical stack on mobile (<768px)

### Demo Page Migration
11. Existing component demo page moved from `/` to `/demo`
12. Demo page remains functional at new route
13. No changes to demo page content (just route change)

### General Requirements
14. Mobile-first responsive design (works on 360px width)
15. Uses existing theme colors from `src/styles/theme.ts`
16. Page background uses `brand.pageBg` (cream/yellow)
17. Buttons use existing theme variants (solid primary, outline secondary)
18. No authentication required
19. No Web3/wallet prompts
